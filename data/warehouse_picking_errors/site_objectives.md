# Picking Accuracy Objectives — RDC-7, FY26

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional organisation, fictional numbers. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: site-leadership. Last updated: 2026-01-20.

Ashgrove Regional Distribution Centre (RDC-7) serves 148 stores and employs 340 picking associates across three shifts: day (Shift A), twilight (Shift B) and night (Shift C).

## Annual goals

- Reduce the site mispick rate to 2.0 per 1,000 lines picked by the end of FY26.
- Reduce store discrepancy claims by 25%.
- Hold cost per line picked flat against the FY25 exit rate.

## Current position

The current site mispick rate is 2.6 per 1,000 lines. The network tolerance is 4.0 per 1,000 lines, so the site is not currently an escalation risk.

Mispicks are the largest single source of store discrepancy claims.

## Priorities and constraints

Cost per line picked is the binding constraint for FY26. Any change that adds time to the pick cycle must be offset by a saving elsewhere, or approved by the regional finance partner before it is rolled out.

Picking accuracy is a stated regional priority following the 2025 store-satisfaction review.

## Out of scope for FY26

Inbound receiving, transport scheduling and the regional returns programme are run by separate teams and are not part of the picking accuracy programme.
