## Monitoring of associates

Any camera or sensor system capable of identifying an individual associate requires works-council consultation before installation. Consultation currently takes eight to twelve weeks.

Pick-accuracy data attributed to a named individual may not be used in performance management until the agreed accuracy-dispute process is in place. That process has not been agreed.

## Recorded footage

Where video is captured on the pick or pack line, retention is capped at 30 days, after which it must be deleted rather than archived.

## Capital approval

Capital expenditure above $250,000 requires regional capital committee approval. The committee meets quarterly and the next submission window closes 2026-09-15.

## Food safety

Any slot move involving allergen-segregated or temperature-controlled stock requires a documented food-safety review before the move. Reviews are completed by the site quality team and typically take two weeks.

## Vendor assessment

Any vendor processing associate-identifiable data requires a completed data protection assessment before deployment. Assessments currently take six to eight weeks.
