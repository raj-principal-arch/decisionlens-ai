# Payments Objectives — FY26

Larkmere Market is an online marketplace for independent makers, selling to consumer buyers and to trade buyers who order in bulk.

## Annual goals

- Raise payment authorisation success to 98.0% by the end of FY26.
- Reduce checkout abandonment attributable to payment failure by 25%.
- Hold the cost of payment acceptance flat or lower.

## Current position

Payment authorisation success is 97.4% and has been stable for six quarters. Failed payments are the second-largest source of checkout abandonment after shipping cost.

## How payments work is prioritised

All FY26 payments initiatives are entered on a single ranked backlog. Each is scored on customer reach, revenue impact, and engineering effort, and the highest combined score is worked first. The backlog is reviewed monthly by the payments steering group.

## Priorities and constraints

Gross merchandise value from trade buyers is a stated growth priority for FY26. Trade buyers are a small share of accounts and a large share of basket value.

The cost of payment acceptance is the binding financial constraint. Any proposal that raises the blended acceptance cost requires finance approval before a pilot may begin.

The partner agreement warrants a quarterly authorisation success rate of not less than 96.0% measured across all payment methods.

## Out of scope for FY26

Seller payouts, refunds, and chargeback dispute handling are owned by separate teams and are not part of the payment reliability programme.
