# Governance Requirements — Payments and Checkout

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional policy for a fictional organisation. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: legal-and-payments-compliance. Created 2025-09-08. Last updated 2026-04-30.

## Card data

Primary card numbers must never be persisted in the browser or in application storage. A payment form may be re-presented only from a tokenised reference held by the payment service.

## Changes to the authorisation flow

Any change to the card authorisation flow requires payment-scheme compliance sign-off before release. Sign-off currently takes ten working days from submission of the change record.

## Saved cards

Storing a card for reuse requires explicit consent recorded at the time of saving. Consent given for marketing does not authorise card storage.

## Declines and automated decisions

Where an automated risk decision refuses a customer's order, a documented human-review route must exist. The route must be available regardless of order value.

## New payment vendors

Any new vendor in the payment path requires a completed security assessment. Assessments currently take eight to twelve weeks.

## Experiment data

Experiment datasets containing payment identifiers are deleted 180 days after the experiment closes. Re-analysis is not possible from the original records after that date.
