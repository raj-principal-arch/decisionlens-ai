# Checkout Objectives — FY26

Larkfield Fresh is an online grocery retailer. Customers shop on a web storefront and a mobile app, and orders are delivered from regional depots in the North, Central and South regions.

## Annual goals

- Return the checkout failure rate to 4.0% or below by the end of FY26.
- Raise checkout completion to 78%.
- Hold payment processing cost per order flat or lower.

## Current position

Checkout failure rate has risen from 4.1% in 2025-Q4 to 6.4% in 2026-Q2, a deterioration of more than half in two quarters. This is the sharpest movement in any checkout metric we track.

Checkout reliability is treated as the largest addressable source of lost orders this year.

## Priorities and constraints

Payment processing cost per order is the binding financial constraint. Any proposal that raises it requires finance approval before rollout.

Peak trading runs from mid-September. A change freeze applies across checkout for the duration of the peak window.

## Out of scope for FY26

Fulfilment slot capacity is owned by the fulfilment planning team and is not part of the checkout reliability programme. The substitutions quality programme is likewise run separately.
