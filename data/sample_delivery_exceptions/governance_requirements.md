# Governance Requirements — Delivery

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional policy for a fictional organisation. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: legal-privacy. Last updated: 2026-03-15.

## Personal data

Delivery photographs are personal data. Retention is capped at 90 days, after which they must be deleted rather than archived.

## Data residency

Customer address data must remain within its region of origin. Cross-region processing requires a documented transfer assessment approved before launch.

## Customer messaging

Automated delivery messaging requires recorded opt-in consent. Consent is not transferable between message categories: marketing opt-in does not authorise delivery notifications.

## AI-generated content

Any AI-generated content sent to a customer requires human review before dispatch. This requirement applies regardless of the model's stated confidence and has no volume exemption.

## Driver data

Driver location data may be used for routing and dispatch only. Use for individual performance evaluation requires works-council consultation.

## Vendor assessment

Any new vendor processing customer data requires a completed data protection assessment. Assessments currently take six to eight weeks.
