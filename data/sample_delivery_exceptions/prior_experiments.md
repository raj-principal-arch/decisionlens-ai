# Prior Experiments — Delivery Exceptions

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional experiments and fictional results. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: research. Last updated: 2024-11-20.

## Pre-arrival notification pilot (September–October 2024)

Pre-arrival SMS was enabled for a subset of deliveries in two metro areas.

First-attempt success in the pilot group was 88.1%, against 87.6% in the comparison group.

The pilot had no randomised control group. The comparison group was selected after the fact from deliveries in the same period. The difference is not statistically meaningful, and the notification effect cannot be separated from seasonal variation.

## Customer survey (October 2024)

80% of surveyed customers said they want SMS alerts before arrival.

The survey was sent to 15 customers, all of whom had already opted into SMS marketing.

## Address correction spot-check (November 2024)

A manual review of 200 failed deliveries in one metro area found that 41% had an incomplete or incorrect delivery address.

Corrections were applied by hand for those orders. No follow-up measurement was taken, so the effect of correction on first-attempt success is unknown.

## Not attempted

No experiment has tested driver-side workflow changes, and none has tested automated exception triage.
