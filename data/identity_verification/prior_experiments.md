## Document capture redesign (March–April 2026)

A redesigned document capture step was tested against the existing step with random assignment at application start. 24,000 applications per arm.

Document capture drop-off was 26.9% in the treatment arm against 31.4% in the control arm. The difference holds at conventional significance.

The test measured drop-off at the capture step only. It did not measure whether the applications that continued went on to open accounts. The redesign has not been rolled out; the flag remains at 0%.

## Manual review status updates (January 2026)

Applicants waiting in the manual review queue were sent a status message at 24 hours and again at 72 hours.

Abandonment among messaged applicants was 64% against 68% among applicants in the same period who were not messaged. Assignment was not random: messages were sent only to applicants who had supplied a mobile number.

## Liveness capture pilot (February 2026)

Liveness capture was offered to 2,140 applicants recruited from the customer beta panel. 1,893 of them, or 88.5%, completed the capture.

Consent was captured through the standard terms-and-conditions acceptance at account creation. No standalone disclosure was presented and no destruction schedule was published.

The pilot cohort was drawn from a panel that had opted into product testing. Applicants who had already abandoned were not eligible for the panel.

Compliance review of the pilot opened on 2026-06-18 and has not concluded. The 1,893 templates collected remain in storage pending that review.

## Not attempted

No experiment has tested changing the manual review routing rules. No experiment has tested automated decisioning of any kind, and no back-test of the Veriphex score against this applicant population has been run.
