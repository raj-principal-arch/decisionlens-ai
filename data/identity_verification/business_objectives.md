Larkspur Financial Services is the regulated banking arm of Larkspur Retail Group. It offers the Larkspur Spend Account to retail customers, opened online or in the mobile application.

## Annual goals

- Open 500,000 new Spend Accounts in FY27.
- Reduce verification abandonment to below 25% by the end of FY27.
- Grow the share of new accounts opened by applicants aged 18 to 24 to 30%.
- Hold cost per opened account flat or lower.

## Current position

Verification abandonment currently sits at 38%. Identity verification is the largest single source of lost applications.

Applicants aged 18 to 24 are the fastest-growing applicant group and the slowest to convert.

## Priorities and constraints

Speed of onboarding is the stated FY27 differentiator. Leadership has asked for a "minutes not days" experience.

Regulatory standing is a board-level commitment. The remediation programme agreed with the supervisor in March 2026 takes precedence over growth work wherever the two conflict.

No incremental vendor spend is approved before the Q3 planning review on 2026-09-15.

## Out of scope for FY27

Card issuance, the rewards programme, and merchant dispute handling are owned by separate teams and are not part of the onboarding programme.
