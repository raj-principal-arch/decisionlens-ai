# Regulatory and Contractual Obligations — Account Opening

> The requirements below are binding on the account-opening journey. They are not preferences and they are not tradeable against delivery dates. Where a proposal conflicts with one of them, the proposal changes.

## Record retention

Identity verification records must be retained for five years after the account relationship ends. A record comprises the submitted document images, the verification outcome, and the reasons recorded for that outcome.

Deleting document images once verification succeeds is not permitted, whatever the storage saving or the privacy argument for it.

## Adverse action

Any application that is declined, withdrawn on risk grounds, or approved on materially worse terms must receive a written statement of the specific principal reasons for that outcome within 30 calendar days.

"The automated system declined it" is not a specific principal reason. An outcome the organisation cannot explain in specific terms is an outcome it may not issue.

## Automated decisioning

No application may be declined solely by an automated process. A named human reviewer must record their own decision and the basis for it before any decline is issued. This requirement has no confidence threshold and no volume exemption.

## Biometric information

Facial geometry captured for liveness detection or face matching is biometric information.

Consent must be obtained through a standalone written disclosure presented before capture. Consent bundled into general terms of service is not valid consent.

A written retention and destruction schedule must be published before the first collection, not after.

Biometric identifiers must be destroyed within one year of the end of the account relationship.

## Model risk and the November review

Under the remediation letter of 2026-03-09 the organisation committed to an external model-risk review on 2026-11-16. The date is fixed and has been notified to the supervisor.

Every model that contributes to an account-opening decision must be entered in the model inventory, with completed validation evidence, no later than 30 days before that review — that is, by 2026-10-17. A model not in the inventory by that date may not be in the decision path on the review date.

## Vendor assessment

Any new vendor that processes applicant identity data requires a completed data protection assessment before it receives production traffic. Assessments currently take nine to eleven weeks.
