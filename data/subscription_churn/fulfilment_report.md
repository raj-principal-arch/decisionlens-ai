## Menu structure

The weekly menu carries 14 recipes and rotates on an eight-week cycle.

Weeks one and two of a new subscription are served from a fixed starter menu of six best-selling recipes. The starter menu is identical for every new subscriber and sits outside the rotation. A new subscriber sees their first rotation week in week three at the earliest.

Menu selection closes at 18:00 on the Thursday five days before delivery. Subscribers who do not choose receive the default box for that week.
