# Governance Requirements — Subscription

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional policy for a fictional organisation. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: legal-privacy. Last updated: 2026-04-08.

## Payment credentials

Stored card credentials may be re-attempted only within card-network rules: a maximum of four automated retry attempts per billing cycle, and no retry after a hard decline. Manual re-attempt by an agent requires the subscriber's recorded authorisation.

## Post-cancellation contact

Post-cancellation marketing requires a separate retention-contact consent. Consent to receive subscription service messages does not authorise win-back campaigns.

## Dietary and allergen data

Dietary preference and allergen data is special-category data. It may be used for order fulfilment and menu filtering only. It may not be used for marketing segmentation and may not be shared with a processor outside the fulfilment scope.

## AI-generated content

Recipe descriptions and menu recommendations generated by a model and shown to a subscriber require human review before publication. This applies regardless of the model's stated confidence and has no volume exemption.

## Data retention

Subscriber records are minimised 180 days after cancellation. Order history, menu selections and delivery records are deleted; a hashed identifier and the cancellation date are retained.

## Vendor assessment

Any new processor handling subscriber data requires a completed data protection assessment. Assessments currently take five to seven weeks.
