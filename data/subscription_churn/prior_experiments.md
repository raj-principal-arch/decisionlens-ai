# Prior Experiments — Subscriber Retention

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional experiments and fictional results. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: research. Last updated: 2026-05-30.

## Expanded menu pilot (February–April 2026)

A randomised subset of subscribers was shown 22 weekly recipes instead of 14, for eight weeks.

Day-90 retention was 71.2% in the test arm against 70.4% in the control arm, with 1,900 subscribers per arm. The difference is not statistically meaningful.

The pilot excluded subscribers in their first 30 days by design, so as not to disturb the onboarding sequence. Nothing in the result speaks to early-tenure subscribers.

## First-box discount depth test (June–August 2025)

Two acquisition offers were run in parallel: 50% off the first box, and 30% off the first box.

The 50% offer acquired 34% more subscribers per unit of acquisition spend. Day-21 retention was 71.9% for the 50% arm and 78.3% for the 30% arm.

Cost per retained subscriber at day 90 was never calculated for either arm.

## Win-back email test (October 2025)

A win-back email sent 14 days after cancellation reactivated 3.1% of recipients. The test ran for four weeks and was not repeated.

Recipients were drawn only from account-page cancellations. The marketing platform receives no cancellation event for subscriptions closed on payment failure, so that group could not be contacted.

## Not attempted

No test has been run on the dunning or card-update sequence.

No test has been run on any aspect of the first-box experience.

No test has measured whether menu breadth affects subscribers in their first month.
