# Cancellation Exit Survey — 2026-Q2 Analysis

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional research on a fictional subscription service. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: research. Last updated: 2026-07-18.

## Method

214 free-text responses were collected between 2026-04-01 and 2026-06-30 and coded independently by two researchers into six reason categories. Inter-coder agreement was 0.81.

The exit survey is presented as the final step of the account-page cancellation flow. Completion is optional and the subscriber can close the page without answering.

The survey is a page rendered by the account front end. It has no email, SMS or telephone equivalent, and the research team cannot trigger it from any other system.

Responses are unweighted. The instrument has not changed since it was introduced in 2025-Q1.

## Headline finding

Recipe variety is the dominant reason subscribers give for leaving. It accounts for 41.6% of coded responses, more than twice the next category.

Price or value follows at 19.2%. Delivery and packaging complaints are comparatively rare at 8.4%.

## Representative verbatims

"The menu started to feel like the same six dinners on a loop."

"I could predict what would be on the list before I opened the email."

"We loved it for a while. Then it was chicken traybake again."

"Nothing wrong with the food. It just stopped being interesting."

"Cancelling was easy at least. The page took ten seconds."

## What this analysis does not cover

The export records tenure at cancellation for every respondent. The reason shares above are pooled across all tenures; the two fields have never been cross-tabulated.

The coded categories describe what people wrote. Nothing in this analysis links a stated reason to anything the subscriber did.

## Recommendation from research

Menu breadth should be treated as the leading hypothesis for FY26 retention work.
