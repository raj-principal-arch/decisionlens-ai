## About this programme

Saltbrook Kitchen sells a weekly meal-kit subscription. Subscribers choose from a rotating recipe menu each week and receive a box of pre-portioned ingredients.

## Annual goals

- Reduce monthly subscriber churn to 5.5% by the end of FY26.
- Raise day-90 retention of new subscribers to 60%.
- Hold cost per retained subscriber flat or lower.

## Current position

Monthly churn improved to 6.9% in Q1 after two quarters of increase, and the FY26 target of 5.5% remains achievable if the trend holds.

Churn, not acquisition, is the constraint on growth this year. Acquisition volume has grown in every quarter since 2025-Q2.

## Priorities and constraints

Cost per retained subscriber is the binding constraint this year. Any proposal that raises it requires finance approval before a pilot may begin.

Subscriber trust in delivery reliability and menu quality is a stated board-level priority for FY26.

## Out of scope for FY26

The headline weekly price is set by finance on an annual cycle and may not be changed by the retention programme. Promotional depth on the first box remains owned by growth.
