## D-2026-01 — Own-brand everyday low pricing

**Decision:** move own-brand health and household lines to everyday low pricing, funded from the promotional budget, effective 5 January 2026.

**Rationale:** own-brand price perception was the largest driver of basket switching in the FY26 trading review. The own-brand earn rate was reduced from 4 points per pound to 1 point per pound to fund the price reduction, and till coupon printing was retired at the same time.
