# Care Club Member Research — Wave 3

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional research, fictional participants, fictional numbers. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: insight. Fieldwork: 2 October to 10 November 2023. Report issued: 2023-11-28.

## Method

Online survey of 1,204 Care Club members, quota-sampled against the member base as at September 2023 by age band, region and store format. Response rate 22%.

Followed by 42 depth interviews in six stores across three regions, deliberately including 14 members who had not scanned their card in the previous six months.

Sampling and weighting were reviewed by the insight team's methods lead. Confidence intervals are reported in the appendix, which is held in the insight archive and is not reproduced here.

## What members said motivates scanning

- 68% named money off everyday health and household lines as their main reason for scanning.
- 22% named personalised reminders about repeat purchases and seasonal health products.
- 7% named being able to see their spending history.
- 3% gave another reason or none.

Earning rate was the strongest stated driver in every age band and in every region.

## Friction

Only 3 of the 42 interviewees could find their points balance in the app without prompting. Eleven abandoned the attempt.

Members described the points balance screen as buried and the offers list as a wall of things they do not buy.

## Redemption behaviour

79% of redemptions in the survey period were made with a coupon printed at the till. 14% were made in the app. 7% were applied manually by a colleague at the counter.

Members preferred the till coupon because, as one put it, it is in your hand and you cannot forget it.

## Recommendations

1. Increase points earned per pound on own-brand health and household lines, which members recognise and buy repeatedly.
2. Simplify the app so that the points balance is visible on the first screen.
3. Keep the till coupon as the default redemption route.
