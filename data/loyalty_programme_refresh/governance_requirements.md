# Governance Requirements — Care Club

## Dispensing and prescription data

Dispensing records, prescription history and any data originating in the dispensary system may not be used for marketing segmentation, targeting or personalisation. Care Club membership consent does not cover this use. A separate, explicit, purpose-specific consent is required and must be recorded per member.

## Health inference from purchase data

Inferring a health condition from purchase history is treated as special-category processing. Any feature that does so requires a completed data protection impact assessment, approved before launch. Assessments currently take six weeks from submission.

## Prize draws and instant-win mechanics

Any mechanic with a chance element requires a promotions licence. Current lead time from application to approval is ten to twelve weeks.

## Changes to earn or redeem value

Any change to the rate at which points are earned, or to the value at which they are redeemed, must be notified to members at least 30 days before it takes effect.

## Member communications

A member communication that references a health condition, a symptom or a medicine requires pharmacist sign-off before dispatch. This applies to automated and AI-generated content and has no volume exemption.

## Vendors

A new vendor processing member data requires an executed data processing agreement. Current lead time is eight to ten weeks.
