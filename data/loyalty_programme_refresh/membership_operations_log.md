## 2025-04-22 — Pellworth account migration

251,000 Pellworth Chemists member accounts were loaded into the Care Club database and became addressable the same day. Balances were converted at the agreed rate. Migrated accounts were counted in the active-member base from the date of load.

## 2025-10-15 — Dormant account closure

Accounts with no recorded earn or redeem event in the preceding 24 months were closed, counting activity in the predecessor programme for migrated accounts. The active-member base fell from 2,646,000 to 1,728,000 on the day of the run. 918,000 accounts were closed, of which 214,000 were accounts migrated from Pellworth Chemists in April.

The run was requested to reduce mailing cost and to bring the database within the retention policy. Reporting was not restated. The engagement rate series in the analytics export uses the closing active-member base for each period.

## 2025-11-12 — App rebuild release

The rebuilt Brackenhill app replaced the previous app for all members. Push notification consent could not be carried across and was re-collected in the new app. Recorded opt-in fell from 61.0% to 23.0% and stood at 34.2% at 30 June 2026.

No usability study has been run against the rebuilt app.

## 2026-01-05 — Till coupon printing retired

Coupon printing at the till was switched off in all stores on the same day the own-brand price change took effect. Redemption is now available in the app or by colleague override at the counter.

## Standing issue — dispensing counter scans

Loyalty scans taken at the pharmacy dispensing counter run on the separate dispensary till system and are not written back to the member account. Engagement counts therefore exclude any member activity that happens only at that counter. The volume is not known.

## 2026-03-03 — Contact centre telephony migration

Care Club contact centre telephony was migrated to a new supplier. Average answer time improved from 4m 10s to 1m 55s. No change was made to member-facing programme mechanics.
