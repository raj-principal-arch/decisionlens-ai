Norvane Electronics sells consumer electronics online and through 140 retail stores. Customer support runs five queues: Orders & Delivery, Returns & Refunds, Technical Faults, Billing & Payments, and Account & Access.

## Annual goals

- Reduce median time-to-resolution for customer support tickets from 21 hours to 12 hours by the end of FY26.
- Reduce p90 time-to-resolution below 60 hours.
- Hold support cost per ticket flat.

## Current position

Ticket volume is up 16.7% year over year, from 35,900 to 41,880 per quarter. Routing accuracy sits at 78%. Reassignment churn is the complaint raised most often in the monthly support review.

## Priorities and constraints

Support headcount is frozen for FY26. Any proposal that requires additional agents must be funded by a reduction elsewhere and approved by finance before a pilot begins.

Cost per ticket is a board-reported measure this year.

## Out of scope for FY26

Retail store operations, warranty administration and the repair network are handled by separate teams and are not part of the support responsiveness programme.
