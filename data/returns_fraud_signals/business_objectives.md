# Returns Integrity Programme — FY27 Objectives

Alderwick Outfitters operates 118 specialty outdoor stores and one e-commerce channel. The
fiscal year matches the calendar year, so FY27 runs January to December 2026.

## Why this programme exists

Returns abuse costs the business $4.2M a year. The rise in return rate is the clearest
signal we have that returns abuse is increasing, and it has risen in every quarter since
the start of FY26.

Return rate is the programme's headline measure of returns abuse. It is reported weekly to
the merchandising leadership team and is the metric against which this programme will be
judged.

## FY27 goals

- Reduce the total return rate from 14.8% to 11.5% by the end of FY27.
- Recover $2.5M of the $4.2M in annual losses attributable to returns abuse.
- Hold returns processing cost per unit flat or lower.

## Constraints on the programme

Free returns within 90 days remains a brand commitment for FY27 and will not be changed.
The commitment was reaffirmed by the board in January 2026 and is written into the loyalty
programme terms.

Any new recurring spend above $50,000 requires CFO approval before a contract is signed.

FY27 adds no headcount to loss prevention. Whatever is chosen has to be run by the teams
that exist.

## Out of scope

Supplier quality and size-chart governance sit with Merchandising and are outside the
Returns Integrity programme's remit.

Warehouse and fulfilment operations are run by Supply Chain and are not part of this
programme.
