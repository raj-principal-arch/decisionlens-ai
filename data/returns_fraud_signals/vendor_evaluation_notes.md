## What the product is

A hosted scoring service that assigns each returning customer a risk score and recommends
an action at the point of return: accept, inspect, or refuse.

"Clients see a 23% reduction in returns fraud within two quarters of deployment."

The deck does not define returns fraud, does not state a baseline, does not name a client,
and does not describe how the reduction was measured. Asked for the underlying study, the
account team offered to "follow up".

"The model identifies high-risk returners with 91% precision."

Asked what the model is scored against, the account team said "confirmed cases". Alderwick
has no confirmed cases.

Against Alderwick's stated $4.2M annual abuse loss, Orlin projects payback within five
months. The payback calculation uses our figure, not theirs.

## Deployment requirements

Orlin requires a nightly feed of 24 months of unified customer purchase and return history,
keyed to a single customer identifier across all channels.

Asked how the platform performs where a customer's store and online activity cannot be
linked, the account team said accuracy "degrades gracefully". No figure was offered.
