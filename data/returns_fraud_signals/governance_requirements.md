# Governance Requirements — Returns and Customer Data

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional policy for a fictional organisation. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: legal-privacy. Author: Kim Sadler, Counsel. Created: 2025-09-01. Last updated: 2026-05-20.

## Automated decisions about customers

A return may not be refused, restricted, or fee-charged on the basis of an automated score
without documented human review and a stated appeal path. The reviewer must be able to see
and override the score.

Any customer whose return is refused must be given the reason in writing at the time of
refusal.

## Customer-level indicators at the point of service

Customer-level risk indicators must not be displayed to store associates at the point of
service. Where an inspection is required, it must be triggered by a property of the item or
the transaction, not by a characterisation of the customer.

## Personal data and processors

Customer purchase and return history is personal data. Engaging a new processor requires a
completed data protection assessment before any data is transferred. Assessments currently
take six to eight weeks.

Returns and purchase data may not be transferred outside its region of origin, and may not
be used to train a model hosted outside that region.

## In-store video

In-store video is retained for 30 days and may be used for incident investigation only.
Building a profile of an identified customer from video is prohibited.

## Loyalty programme terms

The loyalty programme terms guarantee free returns within 90 days. Any change to those
terms requires 30 days' notice to members and sign-off from legal before the notice is
issued.

## Record keeping

Where a return is refused, the record of the refusal and its stated reason must be retained
for 24 months and must be producible on request by the customer.
