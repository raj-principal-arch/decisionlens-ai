## D-2025-08 — Receipt-required returns trial, rolled back

**Decision:** roll back the receipt-required returns trial in the four District 4 pilot
stores.

**Rationale:** the trial ran from 6 October to 24 November 2025. It was rolled back after
customer complaints and two escalations to the regional director. No returns data was
collected during the trial window, and no comparison stores were selected. The four stores
were also midway through a remodel programme that reduced footfall over the same period, so
any change in return volumes cannot be separated from the drop in traffic.

**Owner:** returns-operations. **Date:** 2025-12-03.

## D-2026-01 — Restocking fee not approved

**Decision:** do not introduce a restocking fee on apparel returns.

**Rationale:** free returns is a brand commitment and the loyalty terms guarantee it. The
proposal was rejected on that basis without a cost-benefit review, so no estimate exists of
what a fee would save or what it would cost in lost sales.

**Owner:** product-leadership. **Date:** 2026-01-27.

## D-2026-03 — Returns data mart deferred to FY28

**Decision:** defer the returns data mart from the FY27 capital plan to FY28.

**Rationale:** capital was allocated to the store point-of-sale refresh instead. Until the
returns data mart is delivered, customer return history cannot be joined across store and
online channels, and no per-customer view of returns exists in any system.

**Owner:** data-platform. **Date:** 2026-03-16.

## D-2026-04 — Online returns portal retained

**Decision:** renew the contract with the Larkspur returns portal for online return
initiation.

**Rationale:** migration cost was judged higher than the benefit. Alderwick receives a nightly
summary file from Larkspur; item-level detail is available from 2026-Q1 onwards only, and
nothing earlier can be reconstructed.

**Owner:** e-commerce. **Date:** 2026-04-09.
