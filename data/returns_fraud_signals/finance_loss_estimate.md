# Returns Abuse — Annual Loss Estimate

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional finance memo, fictional numbers. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: finance-fpa. Author: Sam Ortiz, FP&A. Created: 2026-02-18. Last updated: 2026-06-30.

## Headline

Estimated annual loss from returns abuse: **$4.2M**.

## How the figure was produced

The estimate applies the Specialty Retail Council's 2024 published estimate that 5.9% of
returned merchandise value is attributable to abusive returns to Alderwick's FY26 refund
total of $71.2M.

The Council reports a range of 1.5% to 8.8% across its member retailers. Applied to the
same base, the plausible range is $1.1M to $6.3M. The midpoint has been used for planning
because a single number was requested for the FY27 plan.

## What this figure is not

The Council does not publish its methodology, its member composition, or how abuse was
identified in the underlying data. We do not know whether its members resemble Alderwick in
assortment, price point, channel mix, or returns policy.

No internal measurement of abusive returns exists. Nothing in Alderwick's systems
distinguishes an abusive return from a legitimate one, so the 5.9% has not been checked
against anything of our own.

The estimate is a benchmark transposed onto our refund total. It is not a measurement of
Alderwick's losses and should not be quoted as one.

## Request outstanding

A request to fund a physical inspection audit of a random sample of returned units was
submitted to the FY27 planning round in March 2026. It was not scheduled. Estimated cost
was $60,000 for a single-quarter sample across eight stores and the returns hub.
