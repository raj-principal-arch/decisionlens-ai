> These are recorded statements from a review call. They are what people said, not what has

Attendees: Priya Raman (Chief Merchant), Dana Okonkwo (Director, Returns Operations),
Aaron Bexley (District Manager, District 4), Marcus Delaney (Store Manager, Fairhaven,
store 042), Teresa Vogel (Store Manager, Camden Row, store 087), Nell Faraday (Regional
Loss Prevention Lead), Jules Amari (Analytics).

## How the examples were gathered

Ahead of the call, Dana Okonkwo sent a request to all 118 store managers asking for recent
examples of returns they believed were abusive. Two managers replied. Both are on this
call. No structured template was used and no store was asked to review a defined sample of
returns.

## Priya Raman, Chief Merchant

"Return rate is the number I watch. It has gone up six quarters running. That is abuse, and
I do not need a study to tell me so."

"We are losing four point two million a year. That is the number in the plan and that is
the number I am accountable for."

## Marcus Delaney, Store Manager, Fairhaven

"We took back four Tarnside shells last month that had clearly been worn. Mud in the
cuffs, and one still had a lift ticket on the zip. Wardrobing is what this is, and I would
bet it is most of what we are seeing."

Asked how many returns his store processed in the same month, Marcus said he did not have
the figure to hand.

## Teresa Vogel, Store Manager, Camden Row

"One customer returned fourteen items in March. Fourteen. I cannot stop her because the
system shows me nothing, and by the policy she has not done anything wrong."

## Aaron Bexley, District Manager, District 4

"It is the same in every district. Marcus and Teresa are just the two who bothered to write
it up."

"When we required receipts in the four pilot stores last autumn the returns stopped almost
overnight. We should never have rolled it back."

## Nell Faraday, Regional Loss Prevention

"I can only see returns rung up in my own stores' point of sale. If someone buys online and
returns in store, or spreads returns across three stores, I have no way to connect it."

"My instinct is that the online channel is far worse than the stores, but I have never been
able to look."

## Dana Okonkwo, Director, Returns Operations

"There is no field for this. An associate who is certain a return is fraudulent processes
it anyway and moves on, and the system records 'changed mind'. Whatever the reason codes
say, they are the customer's words, not ours."

## Open question raised and not answered

Jules Amari asked what share of returns is actually abusive. Nobody on the call had a
number, and no one could say where such a number would come from today.
