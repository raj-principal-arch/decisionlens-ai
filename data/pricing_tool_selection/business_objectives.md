# Fresh Programme Objectives — FY26

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional organisation, fictional numbers. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: product-leadership. Last updated: 2026-01-20.

Larkfield Markets operates 412 convenience stores in three formats: high street, commuter and neighbourhood.

## Annual goals

- Reduce total fresh loss from 6.5% to 5.5% of fresh sales by the end of FY26.
- Hold on-shelf availability at 17:00 at or above 93% in every format.
- Add no hours to the store labour model.

## Current position

Fresh markdown losses currently run at 4.0% of fresh sales. Unsold stock written off adds a further 2.5%. Fresh loss is the largest controllable cost line in the store profit and loss after labour.

## Priorities and constraints

Store colleague hours are capped at the FY25 level. No proposal may add hours to the store labour model.

Any programme requiring more than £250,000 of capital requires board approval and cannot start before the FY27 planning round in November 2026.

Availability is the protected measure. A loss reduction achieved by ordering less is not a win if availability falls below the floor.

## Out of scope for FY26

The forecourt fuel offer, tobacco and alcohol sit outside the fresh programme. Ambient grocery range decisions are handled by the trading team.
