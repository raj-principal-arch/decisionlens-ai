## Halcyon Retail Labs — Halcyon Shelf Intelligence

Between October and December 2025, Halcyon Shelf Intelligence was deployed across all 300 stores of a comparable convenience chain.

Markdown losses in prepared salads and cut fruit fell 22% against the preceding quarter, July to September 2025.

The deployment covered every store in the chain, so results are reported against the chain's own prior performance.

Halcyon Shelf Intelligence consumes item-level remaining shelf life from your inventory system and returns a recommended reduction schedule to the store handheld. No hardware is required.
