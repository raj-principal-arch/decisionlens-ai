# Governance Requirements — Retail Pricing

> **Synthetic document.** Fictional policy for a fictional organisation. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: legal-compliance. Last updated: 2026-04-30.

## Price integrity

The price displayed at the fixture must match the price charged at the till at all times. A price change may not take effect at the till before the shelf edge has been updated.

## Reduction labelling

A reduction label must show the original price alongside the reduced price. No reduction may be applied to an item after its use-by time has passed.

## Authorisation

A reduction greater than 50% of the original price requires store manager authorisation recorded against the till transaction. There is no automated exemption.

## Colleague task systems

Any system that issues work instructions to store colleagues requires consultation with the colleague forum before deployment. Recent consultations have taken between six and twelve weeks.

## Vendor assessment

Any vendor processing colleague or transaction data requires a completed data protection assessment. Assessments currently take eight to ten weeks.

## Tobacco

Tobacco is sold at the price-marked pack price set by the manufacturer. Store-level price variation and promotional mechanics are not permitted on tobacco lines.
