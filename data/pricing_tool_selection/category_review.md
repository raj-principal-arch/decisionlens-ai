## How markdown works today

Stores run one markdown round at 17:00 under decision D-2025-11. Items reaching their final day are reduced by 25%. A further reduction to 50% after 20:00 is permitted at the store manager's discretion and is not part of the standard round.

Markdown compliance is defined as the share of items reaching their final day that carried a reduction label before 18:00. It is measured by handheld scan at the fixture.

A reduction applied late is a deeper reduction, because the remaining selling window is shorter. Late execution therefore raises markdown loss and waste together rather than trading one against the other.

## Seasonality

Markdown on prepared salads and cut fruit is strongly seasonal. The rate peaks in July to September, when volumes are highest and shelf life is shortest, and falls by roughly a quarter to a third in October to December. The pattern has held in each of the last three years.

Chain fresh loss follows the same shape less sharply. The third quarter is the worst quarter of every year.

## Disposition of unsold stock

A food redistribution partnership operates in 68 neighbourhood stores. Collections are made at close and the diverted volume is recorded as waste, not as markdown.
