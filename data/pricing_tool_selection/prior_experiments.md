## Two-stage markdown trial (March to May 2026)

Twelve stores moved from the single 17:00 markdown round to two windows: 25% at 14:00 and 50% at 18:00.

Waste in the trial stores fell from 3.6% to 2.9% of fresh sales over the trial.

No control stores were used. During the same period Ashcombe Foods extended prepared salad shelf life from three days to four across the range, and two of the twelve stores were refitted. The trial cannot separate these effects from the effect of the markdown change.

Markdown loss in the trial stores was not reported separately.

## Colleague markdown-timing observation (June 2026)

Observers visited six stores for one trading day each and found that the markdown round started after 17:30 in four of the six.

The six stores were nominated by the regional manager. They were not selected at random.

## Shelf-life scanning audit (May 2026)

A manual audit of 480 items across eight stores found that 18% carried no scanned expiry record at the fixture.
