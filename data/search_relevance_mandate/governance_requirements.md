# Governance Requirements — Search and Discovery

> **Synthetic policy** written for a fictional organisation. No real Walmart data.
> Owner: Yusuf Marchetti, legal and privacy counsel. Last updated: 2026-02-28.

## Search query data

Search query text is customer behavioural data. It is retained for 180 days and may not be joined to loyalty identity without a documented lawful basis.

## Data residency

Search query logs may not leave their region of origin. Any processing outside the region requires a transfer assessment approved before launch.

## Personalised ranking

Ranking that varies by identified customer is personalisation and requires recorded consent. Consent coverage is currently 22% of logged-in customers.

## Automated ranking transparency

Any automated system that determines which products a customer is shown must carry a documented description of its ranking factors, reviewable by merchandising on request. This applies to learned ranking models and there is no exemption for model complexity.

## Merchandising control

Merchandising retains the right to pin and to demote individual products for commercial and product-safety reasons. Any ranking system must preserve manual override.

## Vendors

A new vendor processing search query text requires a completed data protection assessment. Assessments currently take eight to ten weeks.
